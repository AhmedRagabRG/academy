# Design Context

## Target Audience

Internal employees of Alsalam Academy, including future administrators, executives, branch
managers, finance, marketing, customer service, and admissions teams.

## Primary Use Cases

Employees use the platform for dependable, daily education-operations work: navigation,
approvals, records, forms, tables, reporting, and future automation.

## Brand Personality

Premium and modern educational operations. The interface communicates professionalism, trust,
education, growth, career development, simplicity, and reliability. Navy is dominant; gold is a
rare accent. Layouts are clean, spacious, corporate, restrained, and RTL-native.

## Mandatory Visual System

- Navy `#0B2A4A`
- Professional blue `#1F5A8A`
- Accent gold `#C9A34E`
- Background gray `#F5F7FA`
- Dark text `#1D2939`
- Arabic headings/body: Alexandria Bold/Regular
- English headings/body: Montserrat SemiBold/Regular
- Lucide outline icons
- Soft borders and shadows with restrained corner radii
